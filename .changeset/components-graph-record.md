`ComponentsGraph`, the graph-registry record class of a package's component index graph (`core:ComponentsGraph`, beside `ShapesGraph`), with the record fields `package`, `version`, `commit` and `sources`; the new `core:commit` term (`coreOntology.commit`, `core.ttl`) records the commit a graph's content was built from.
