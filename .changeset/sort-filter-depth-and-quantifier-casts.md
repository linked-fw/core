A filter further down a sort path or a projection keeps every instance: `orderBy((p) => p.bestFriend.friends.where(…).hobby)` no longer throws, and an instance without a best friend (or without a friend the filter keeps) sorts last; `select((p) => p.bestFriend.friends.where(…).name)` no longer drops the persons without a best friend. A filter before a cast (`p.friends.where(…).as(Employee)`) is kept (it was dropped). DSL-JSON 1.8: a quantifier on a cast relation keeps its cast (`{"pets.as(Dog).some": {"guardDogLevel": 2}}`, was `pets.some` and failed to decode), condition and `sortBy` paths carry `as(X)` casts as fields do, and a `sortBy` filter may name any segment (`whereIndex`; an index that names no segment is refused, in a field too, where it was dropped). Decoding: a quantifier's condition reads the member shape the DSL reads (`sh:node`, the shape targeting `sh:class`, the cast, else core's root `Shape`), never the outer shape; a quantifier may be any operand of `and`/`or`; an empty condition `{}` and an unknown cast label are refused. `not().not()` on a chained quantifier gives the condition back. `FieldSet.pathToStringWithCasts` and `FieldSet.walkPathWithCasts` moved to the internal `castPaths` module.
